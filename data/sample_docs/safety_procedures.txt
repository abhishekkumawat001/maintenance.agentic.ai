INDUSTRIAL SAFETY PROCEDURES
==============================

1. LOCKOUT/TAGOUT (LOTO) PROCEDURES
-------------------------------------

Purpose: Prevent unexpected energization of equipment during maintenance.

LOTO Steps:
1. NOTIFY all affected personnel of the planned lockout
2. SHUT DOWN equipment using normal operating controls
3. ISOLATE all energy sources:
   - Electrical: Open breaker, remove fuses
   - Mechanical: Block, chain, or pin moving parts
   - Hydraulic/Pneumatic: Bleed stored energy, block cylinders
   - Thermal: Allow cooldown or drain hot fluids
   - Chemical: Close and blank valves, drain lines
4. APPLY personal lock and tag at each isolation point
5. VERIFY zero energy state:
   - Try to start equipment (ensure it will not)
   - Test with voltage tester for electrical isolation
   - Check pressure gauges read zero
   - Verify mechanical components cannot move
6. PERFORM the maintenance work
7. REMOVE locks and tags — only the person who applied them
8. RESTORE energy sources and restart equipment
9. NOTIFY all personnel that equipment is back in service

Group LOTO: When multiple workers are involved, each person applies their own lock. A lockbox may be used for complex lockouts.

2. PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT (PPE)
----------------------------------------

Minimum PPE for industrial maintenance:
- Hard hat (ANSI Z89.1)
- Safety glasses with side shields (ANSI Z87.1)
- Steel-toe or composite-toe boots (ASTM F2413)
- Hearing protection in areas > 85 dB (earplugs or earmuffs)
- Work gloves appropriate for the task

Task-Specific PPE:
- Chemical handling: Chemical splash goggles, face shield, chemical-resistant gloves, apron
- Welding/cutting: Welding helmet (auto-darkening), leather gloves, flame-resistant clothing
- Electrical work: Insulated gloves rated for voltage class, arc flash suit (category per NFPA 70E)
- Confined space: Full body harness, gas detector, supplied air respirator if needed
- Grinding: Face shield over safety glasses, leather gloves
- Working at heights: Full body harness with shock-absorbing lanyard, hard hat with chin strap

3. HOT WORK PERMIT PROCEDURES
-------------------------------

Hot Work includes: Welding, cutting, grinding, brazing, soldering, or any spark-producing activity.

Requirements:
1. Obtain Hot Work Permit signed by authorized personnel
2. Inspect area within 35 feet (11 meters) radius for combustibles
3. Remove or protect combustible materials (fire blankets, wet down)
4. Ensure fire extinguisher is present and accessible
5. Test atmosphere for flammable gases (LEL < 10%)
6. Post fire watch during work and for minimum 60 minutes after
7. Fire watch must have communication device and know alarm location

4. CONFINED SPACE ENTRY
-------------------------

Confined spaces include: Tanks, vessels, pits, manholes, silos, hoppers.

Requirements:
1. Obtain Confined Space Entry Permit
2. Atmospheric testing BEFORE entry:
   - Oxygen: 19.5% - 23.5%
   - LEL (flammable gas): < 10%
   - Toxic gases (H2S, CO): Below PEL
3. Continuous atmospheric monitoring during entry
4. Attendant stationed outside at all times
5. Rescue plan and equipment available
6. Communication system between entrant and attendant
7. Mechanical ventilation if atmosphere is not acceptable
8. LOTO all energy sources to the confined space

5. WORKING AT HEIGHTS
-----------------------

Fall protection required at heights > 6 feet (1.8 meters):
- Full body harness with shock-absorbing lanyard or SRL
- Anchor point rated for 5,000 lbs per worker
- Inspect fall protection equipment before each use
- Barricade area below elevated work
- Use tool lanyards to prevent dropped objects
- Scaffolding must be inspected by competent person
- Ladder safety: 4:1 ratio, extend 3 feet above landing, secure at top

6. EMERGENCY PROCEDURES
-------------------------

Fire:
1. Activate fire alarm
2. Call emergency number
3. Evacuate area using nearest safe exit
4. Fight fire ONLY if trained and fire is small
5. Meet at designated assembly point

Chemical Spill:
1. Evacuate immediate area
2. Notify supervisor and emergency response team
3. Consult Safety Data Sheet (SDS) for cleanup procedure
4. Don appropriate PPE before approaching spill
5. Contain spill if safe to do so (absorbent materials, dikes)

Medical Emergency:
1. Call emergency number
2. Do not move injured person unless in immediate danger
3. Administer first aid if trained
4. Keep injured person warm and comfortable
5. Direct emergency responders to location

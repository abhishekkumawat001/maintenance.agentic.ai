PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE CHECKLIST
=================================

This document provides standardized preventive maintenance (PM) checklists for common industrial equipment categories. Each checklist includes inspection frequency, specific tasks, acceptance criteria, and required tools.

1. ROTATING EQUIPMENT (PUMPS, MOTORS, FANS, COMPRESSORS)
---------------------------------------------------------

DAILY CHECKLIST:
□ Check operating temperature (bearing housings, motor frame)
□ Monitor vibration levels (acceptable: < 4.5 mm/s velocity)
□ Listen for abnormal noise (cavitation, bearing noise, rubbing)
□ Verify proper lubrication (oil level, grease condition)
□ Check for leaks (seals, gaskets, piping connections)
□ Record operating parameters (pressure, flow, amperage)
□ Inspect guards and safety devices are in place
□ Verify cooling system operation (fans, water cooling)

WEEKLY CHECKLIST:
□ Check alignment indicators (if installed)
□ Inspect coupling condition
□ Verify foundation bolt tightness
□ Check belt tension and condition (belt-driven equipment)
□ Inspect filters and strainers
□ Review trending data for anomalies

MONTHLY CHECKLIST:
□ Perform vibration measurements and trending
□ Check bearing temperatures with IR thermometer
□ Inspect mechanical seals for excessive leakage
□ Clean cooling fins and ventilation openings
□ Check electrical connections for tightness and corrosion
□ Verify instrument calibration
□ Update maintenance records

QUARTERLY CHECKLIST:
□ Full vibration spectrum analysis
□ Oil sampling and analysis
□ Thermographic inspection
□ Insulation resistance test (motors)
□ Check wear components (wear rings, impeller, liners)
□ Performance test (compare to design conditions)
□ Alignment verification

ANNUAL CHECKLIST:
□ Complete overhaul inspection
□ Replace wear components as needed
□ Re-align all rotating equipment
□ Perform comprehensive electrical testing
□ Update spare parts inventory
□ Review maintenance history and adjust PM intervals

2. HEAT EXCHANGERS
-------------------

MONTHLY:
□ Check tube-side and shell-side pressure drops
□ Monitor approach temperatures
□ Inspect for external leaks
□ Check relief valve operation
□ Record operating parameters

QUARTERLY:
□ Compare heat transfer coefficient to baseline
□ Check for tube fouling (increased pressure drop or reduced heat transfer)
□ Inspect expansion joints and flexible connections
□ Verify corrosion protection systems

ANNUAL:
□ Internal inspection (open and inspect tubes/shell)
□ Tube thickness measurements (UT testing)
□ Clean tubes (chemical cleaning or mechanical cleaning)
□ Inspect gaskets and replace as needed
□ Hydrostatic test after reassembly
□ Update corrosion rate data

3. VALVES AND ACTUATORS
------------------------

MONTHLY:
□ Cycle control valves through full range
□ Check actuator operation (response time, positioning accuracy)
□ Inspect valve packing for leaks
□ Verify instrument air supply pressure
□ Check positioner calibration

QUARTERLY:
□ Stroke test safety relief valves
□ Inspect valve body for erosion/corrosion
□ Check actuator diaphragm or piston condition
□ Verify emergency shutdown valve function
□ Test partial stroke on critical isolation valves

ANNUAL:
□ Full internal inspection of critical valves
□ Replace packing on leaking valves
□ Calibrate all control valves
□ Test and certify safety relief valves
□ Actuator overhaul as needed

4. PIPING AND PRESSURE VESSELS
-------------------------------

QUARTERLY:
□ Visual inspection for corrosion, leaks, supports
□ Check pipe support condition and hanger adjustments
□ Inspect insulation for damage or moisture intrusion
□ Verify expansion joint condition

ANNUAL:
□ Ultrasonic thickness measurements at designated test locations
□ Calculate corrosion rates and remaining life
□ Inspect vessel internals (trays, baffles, nozzles)
□ Test relief devices
□ Review inspection history and schedule repairs

5. ELECTRICAL SYSTEMS
----------------------

MONTHLY:
□ Thermographic scan of switchgear, MCCs, and distribution panels
□ Check for unusual odors or sounds in electrical rooms
□ Verify UPS battery condition and charge level
□ Test emergency lighting

QUARTERLY:
□ Inspect and clean electrical connections
□ Test ground fault protection systems
□ Verify transfer switch operation (generator backup)
□ Check power quality (voltage balance, harmonics)

ANNUAL:
□ Comprehensive insulation resistance testing
□ Protective relay testing and calibration
□ Transformer oil analysis and inspection
□ Arc flash hazard assessment update
□ Breaker testing and maintenance
□ Grounding system integrity test

6. GENERAL GUIDELINES
----------------------

Maintenance Prioritization:
- Priority 1 (Emergency): Safety hazard or imminent failure — immediate action required
- Priority 2 (Urgent): Equipment degraded but operational — schedule within 24-48 hours
- Priority 3 (Planned): Routine maintenance — schedule within normal planning cycle
- Priority 4 (Improvement): Optimization or upgrade — schedule based on resource availability

Documentation Requirements:
- Record all work performed with date, technician name, and findings
- Note any deferred work items with justification
- Update equipment condition assessment
- Report any safety concerns immediately
- Maintain photographic records of significant findings
